## Wallpaper Shortlist

Wallpaper theme extraction uses `material-color-utilities` 0.2.4 (Python bindings to Material Color Utilities, Apache-2.0), with Pillow and NumPy dependencies recorded in `uv.lock`. Packages are installed into the ignored virtual environment, not vendored. Theme colors are computed locally from user-selected images.

Selected from [dusklinux/images](https://github.com/dusklinux/images). Names below are local descriptive labels, not verified artwork titles. At the user's request, the 17 prepared images plus the original Moonlit Mountains (`dark/0012.jpg`) and Pixel Planet (`dark/0008.jpg`) were copied into `shell/Assets/Wallpapers` and registered in the picker. Original creators and redistribution licenses were not established from the collection. Public availability is not a redistribution license; review rights before publishing these assets.

Local evaluation copies are in `.artifacts/wallpapers/`, with a comparison sheet at `.artifacts/wallpaper-shortlist.jpg`. Each theme has three exports:

- `normal`: 1920 x 1080 (16:9).
- `wide`: 2560 x 1080 (the common ultrawide format marketed as 21:9).
- `superwide`: 2560 x 720 (32:9).

Wide versions are crops of the listed originals, not native panoramic versions. They were aspect-cropped and downsampled without upscaling; Rose's crop is shifted downward to retain the dunes. These exports are not native-resolution 3440 x 1440 or 5120 x 1440 wallpapers. All 17 downloaded/exported images were decoded successfully with Qt and their dimensions checked. Source SHA-256 hashes and exact crop rectangles are recorded locally in `.artifacts/wallpaper-shortlist.json`.

For the same composition in different colors, **Abstract Fold** appears in [amber (dark/0003.jpg)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dusklinux/images/main/dark/0003.jpg) and [teal (dark/0005.jpg)](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/dusklinux/images/main/dark/0005.jpg), both 2048 x 1152. The silhouettes and layout visually match; these are existing upstream variants, copied unchanged, not newly generated recolors. A complete five-theme variant set was not verified.

The 19 imported images now appear directly in the wallpaper picker alongside the three original fixtures. Imported selections are saved by file URL, preserving the legacy profile fallback indices. Existing saved selections were not replaced. Twelve additional chat attachments have not been imported because their original files or direct source links were unavailable.
